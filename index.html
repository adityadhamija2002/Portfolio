07 / RETAIL PROFITABILITY

NEXT plc: does online growth improve profit?

Analysed twelve years of audited financial data to test how the migration from stores to Online/Directory sales was associated with segment profit, Group margin and distribution-cost pressure.

Online sales share68.4%
Online profit growth£345.2m

Finding: Online/Directory profit growth more than offset the decline in Retail profit by FY2026, but higher online share was strongly associated with greater distribution-cost intensity (r = 0.985). Growth should be judged through profit contribution, margin quality and cost-to-serve—not sales share alone.
